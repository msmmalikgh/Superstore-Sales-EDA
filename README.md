# 📊 Superstore Sales & Profit EDA

## 📌 Objective
Analyze sales, profit, discounts, and shipping strategies to uncover key business insights.

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📂 Dataset
data/Sample - Superstore

## 🔍 Key Insights
Sales & Profit Insights

Top Performing Categories: Technology leads in total sales, followed closely by Office Supplies and Furniture.

Most Profitable Products: Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier, Fellowes PB500 Electric Punch Comb Binder, and HON 5400 Series Task Chairs are top revenue generators.

Customer Segments: Office Supplies and Technology products consistently drive higher profits.

Regional Performance: West and East regions show the strongest sales and profits, while some Central and South states show mixed profitability.

Shipping & Discounts: High discounts correlate with negative profit margins, and standard shipping is less profitable than other modes.
